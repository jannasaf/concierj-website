Our story

The gap between a beautiful property and a confident guest.

Across years of travel — boutique retreats in the Caribbean, wellness resorts across the United States, safaris and action-based trips in Africa, city-center properties in Europe — the same gap kept showing up.

A romance package inquiry made after hours that never got answered. A group booking lost because no one could clearly explain the accessibility features or dietary accommodations. A remote worker who chose a less beautiful property simply because it confirmed desk space and wifi speed upfront. A package sale that evaporated because two staff members gave conflicting information.

None of that was a failure of hospitality. The properties themselves were exceptional. The failure was in the layer underneath — the questions, the hesitations, the after-hours moments that decide whether a booking happens at all, long before a guest ever arrives.

Guests choose properties they trust, and that trust starts the moment they land on your website. Improve the communication before the booking, and you improve the trust — and the bookings that follow.

Concierj exists to close the infrastructure gap underneath the personalized hospitality that makes boutique properties so beloved.

About the founder

Janna Safran holds a Master of Public Health from Columbia University, focused on systems and infrastructure strengthening — using data to understand how organizations operate, where they break down, and how to fix them.

Having traveled to more than twenty countries with a deep interest in guest experience and behavior, she has felt firsthand the intentionality that defines boutique hospitality — and the pre-booking gap that quietly undermines it. Concierj is the convergence of those two threads: systems thinking applied to an industry she knows personally.
