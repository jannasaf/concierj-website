Dine

Two rooms, one kitchen.

Juniper

Our dining room leans on the coast for its menu — the day's catch, produce from the inland valley, and a wood-fired hearth that runs most nights. Reservations lean toward guests, but we always hold a few tables back.

The Bluff Bar

A low-lit room for the hour between the beach and dinner. Local wine, a short cocktail list built around citrus and salt, and a fireplace that stays lit until the last guest leaves.

Wellness

Slower mornings, on purpose.

The Bathhouse

Two treatment rooms built around the sound of the water below — ocean-mineral soaks, a cedar sauna, and treatments drawn from the coast itself: kelp, salt, and eucalyptus grown on the property.

Sunrise yoga runs most mornings on the lower terrace, weather and tides permitting — no reservation needed, mats provided.

About Azure Bluff

Sixteen rooms, one stretch of coast.

Azure Bluff sits on eleven acres above the Pacific, built small on purpose. There's no ballroom, no conference center — just sixteen rooms, a dining room, a bathhouse, and a lot of coastline. Most of what guests remember happens off-property: a trail, a cove, a vineyard down the road. We just make sure they know it's there.
